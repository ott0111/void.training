Ticket Types
Your first and foremost responsibility as a mod is to efficiently manage the ticket system in Void Esports. We operate with two primary ticket categories, each requiring specific handling protocols.
👥

General Ticket

These are created by community members for support inquiries, moderator assistance, or reporting issues. Handle with professionalism and follow escalation procedures for complex matters.

👤

Roster Ticket

Application channel for users seeking to join Void Esports. Initial response must include age verification and direction to appropriate resources. Follow structured onboarding workflow.

Protocol: All ticket responses should begin with professional greeting and age inquiry. Direct new applicants to the appropriate channels and maintain detailed records of all interactions.

Mod Responsibilities & Commands

⚠️ Warn / Report Command

Staff Commands
Use /warn and /report responsibly. For minor rule violations, issue an appropriate warning and document context. For major or repeated violations, escalate to senior staff. Always include: who, what, where, and proof.
Minimum documentation fields:
  • User: username + user ID
  • Reason: short + clear
  • Evidence: link/screenshot
  • Action: warn/mute/ban/escalate
🧾

Documentation Standard

Every major action must be logged: ticket ID, moderator name, user ID, evidence link, and final outcome. If it isn’t documented, it didn’t happen.

⛔

Blacklist Guidelines

Blacklist is serious: confirm identity, collect proof, and escalate for approval. Do not argue. Do not threaten. Keep it short, professional, and final.

  • User: username
  • User ID: discord ID
  • Reason: underage / fraud / harassment
Closing the Ticket
✅

Successful Close

  • Confirm requirements were met
  • Confirm proof was verified (no edits/crops)
  • State next steps (role assignment / review)
  • Thank them + close professionally
🚫

Denial Close

  • State denial reason briefly
  • No arguing, no debate
  • Offer re-apply conditions if allowed
  • Close cleanly
Rule: If answers change when asked differently, stop intake. If they refuse proof, stop intake. Keep it clean, documented, and professional.
