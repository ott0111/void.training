Void Moderator Training

Welcome to the official Void Esports Moderator Training Portal. This academy is designed to prepare future moderators with the knowledge, professional standards, and ticket handling skills required to join the Void Staff Team as a Trial Moderator.

Step 1 — Read Training

Go through the full moderation training and learn how staff members interact with the community.

Step 2 — Learn Ticket Handling

Understand how to properly respond to support tickets and role applications in a professional way.

Step 3 — Pass Certification

Complete the certification exam to prove you understand Void moderation procedures.

What Trial Moderators Do

Assist members in support tickets
Answer questions from the community
Help guide new members
Maintain professionalism at all times

Professional Expectations

Remain respectful to all users
Never argue with members
Represent Void Esports professionally
Escalate serious issues to senior staff

Ready to Become a Trial Moderator?

Once you complete the training material, take the certification exam. Passing the exam confirms you understand the moderation process and qualifies you to apply for a Trial Moderator position.
