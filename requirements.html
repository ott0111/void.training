Moderator Requirements

Before applying to become a Trial Moderator, applicants must meet the expectations below. These requirements ensure that moderators represent Void Esports professionally and help maintain a positive community environment.

Age Requirement

Applicants must be at least 14 years old
Applicants must be honest about their age
Lying about age results in automatic disqualification

Activity Requirement

Must be active in the Discord server
Must engage with community members
Must be available to assist in tickets

Professional Behavior

Remain respectful to all members
Communicate clearly and professionally
Avoid arguments with community members
Represent Void Esports positively

Things Moderators Must Never Do

Abuse staff permissions
Harass members
Show favoritism
Leak internal information

Community Support

Moderators assist members by answering questions, guiding new users, and maintaining a welcoming environment.

Ticket Handling

Moderators respond to support tickets and guide users through applications or questions about Void Esports.

Rule Enforcement

Moderators ensure that community rules are followed and escalate serious issues to senior staff members.

Important: Being a moderator is a responsibility. Moderators represent Void Esports and must act professionally at all times when interacting with members.

Next Step: Moderator Training

If you meet the requirements above, continue to the training section to learn how moderators handle tickets, interact with users, and manage community situations.
