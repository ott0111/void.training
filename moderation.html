Moderation Guide

This guide teaches how to handle difficult situations, reports, and escalation cases. Moderators represent Void Esports, so every response must be calm, professional, and structured.

Core Moderator Rules

Stay professional in every message
Never argue with members
Be clear, calm, and respectful
Document important situations
Escalate when unsure

What NOT To Do

Do not threaten members
Do not insult or mock members
Do not abuse permissions
Do not “power trip”
Do not leak internal info

Handling Toxic Users

If a user is angry or disrespectful, do not match their energy. Keep it professional and move the conversation to the issue.

Example Response:
Hello. I understand you're frustrated. Please explain the issue and I will do my best to help.

De-escalation Steps

Acknowledge the frustration
Ask for details clearly
Offer a solution or next step
If it continues, escalate to senior staff

Handling Reports

Reports require evidence. Collect the info and escalate if the situation is serious.

Example Response:
Hello! Please provide the user's Discord ID and evidence (screenshots). I will escalate this to senior staff for review.

Information to Collect

User’s Discord ID
What happened (short explanation)
Screenshots / proof
When it happened

When to Escalate

Harassment / bullying
Scams / impersonation
Threats
Underage-related cases
Anything you are unsure about

How to Escalate Properly

Stay professional and calm
Collect evidence and details
Ping senior staff / leadership
Do not take action beyond your role

Representing Void Esports

Your tone, patience, and professionalism reflect the organization. Moderators should make Void look structured, secure, and respectful.

Use complete sentences
Avoid slang in tickets
Be consistent with training rules
Escalate rather than guessing
Reminder: If you are not 100% sure, escalate. It is better to escalate early than to handle a serious case incorrectly.
