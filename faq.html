Frequently Asked Questions

This section answers the most common questions about becoming a Trial Moderator. Read this first before opening a ticket for staff-related questions.

How do I become a Trial Moderator?

Read the training sections, learn ticket handling, complete scenario training, then pass the Certification exam. After that, you may apply through the proper recruitment process.

What is a Trial Moderator?

A Trial Moderator is a probationary staff member who is being evaluated. Trial Mods must follow training standards and show professionalism consistently.

Do I need experience?

Experience helps, but it is not required. We care most about professionalism, maturity, and the ability to follow procedures.

What happens if I fail certification?

You can review the training material and try again. Certification exists to ensure recruits understand how Void moderators operate before they join.

What should I do if I’m unsure in a ticket?

Stay calm, collect information, and escalate the situation to senior staff. Do not guess or take action beyond your role.

Can I joke around in tickets?

No. Tickets require professionalism. You can be friendly, but avoid slang, jokes, or informal greetings like “yo” or “bro”.

How do I handle toxic members?

Do not argue. Acknowledge the frustration, ask for details, and stay professional. Escalate to senior staff if the situation continues.

What if someone is underage?

Underage applicants do not meet requirements. Collect the necessary information and escalate to senior staff for review and proper handling.

Tip: If your question is answered here, do not open a ticket for it. Tickets should be used for actual applications and serious issues.
