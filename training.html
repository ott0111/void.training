Moderator Training

This training section teaches future moderators how to communicate professionally, assist members, and represent the Void Esports community properly.

Professional Greetings

Hello, how may I help you today?
Hello, what can I assist you with today?
Hello! How can I help you?

Unacceptable Responses

Yo
Bro
Sup
What do you want?

Remain Respectful

Moderators should treat every member with respect, even if the user is being difficult.

Stay Calm

Never argue with members. If a situation becomes serious, escalate it to senior staff.

Clear Communication

Moderators should speak clearly and professionally so that users understand the help being provided.

When to Escalate Issues

Harassment or bullying
Serious rule violations
Scams or impersonation
Situations you are unsure about

How to Escalate

Remain calm and professional
Collect necessary information
Notify senior staff
Allow leadership to review the situation

Be Helpful

Moderators exist to help the community. Always aim to guide members and answer their questions.

Be Patient

Some users may not understand immediately. Take time to explain things clearly.

Be Professional

Moderators represent the Void brand. Every message should reflect professionalism.

Important: Moderators represent the Void organization. All communication must remain professional and respectful.
