Scenario Training

These scenarios simulate real situations moderators encounter. Read the user's message, then use the recommended moderator response. Always stay professional and escalate when necessary.

Scenario 1 — Roster Application

User: “I want to join the roster.”

Correct Response:
Hello! What roster are you applying for today? Please send your tracker link and a screenshot of the default dance in lobby.

Scenario 2 — Creative Application

User: “I want to join Creative.”

Correct Response:
Hello! Please send 2–3 clips of your work. The Creative Department will review them shortly.

Scenario 3 — Content Creator

User: “Can I get Content Creator?”

Correct Response:
Hello! Please link your social media accounts below. The Content Department will review them shortly.

Scenario 4 — GFX / VFX

User: “I do gfx/vfx can I join?”

Correct Response:
Hello! Please link your portfolio and examples of your previous work. A GFX/VFX Lead will review it shortly.

Scenario 5 — Report / Serious Issue

User: “Someone is scamming people.”

Correct Response:
Hello! Please provide the user's Discord ID and any evidence you have (screenshots). I will escalate this to senior staff immediately.

Scenario 6 — Underage Applicant

User: “I’m 12 but I can mod.”

Correct Response:
Hello! Thank you for your interest. Unfortunately you do not meet the age requirements for staff roles. This will be reviewed by senior staff.

Scenario 7 — Angry / Toxic User

User: “This server is trash, staff is useless.”

Correct Response:
Hello. I understand you're frustrated. Please explain the issue and I will do my best to help. If needed, I can escalate this to senior staff.

Scenario 8 — Asking for Staff

User: “Can I be a mod?”

Correct Response:
Hello! Please review our Moderator Training and complete the Certification exam first. After passing, you may apply through a ticket.
Reminder: Never argue with members. If a situation becomes serious or unclear, collect information and escalate to senior staff.
