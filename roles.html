Departments & Role Requirements

Moderators must understand what each department does and what proof is required when a member applies in a ticket. Use these sections as your official reference.

Creative Department

Handles creative recruitment and reviews clips/work for creative roles.

Roles: @Creative Academy, @Main Creative
Required Proof: 2–3 clips of their work
Moderator Action: Ping Creative Department for review

Operations Department

Reviews competitive roster applications and ensures requirements are met.

Roles: @Semi-Pro Roster, @Pro Roster
Required Proof: Tracker + default dance screenshot
Moderator Action: Ping @operations for review

Academy Roster

Entry-level competitive roster. Applicants must provide basic proof.

Role: @Academy Roster
Required Proof: Tracker + default dance screenshot
Moderator Action: Verify info is included before escalating

Content Department

Reviews social accounts and content consistency for creator roles.

Role: @Content Creator
Required Proof: Social media links (YouTube/TikTok/X etc.)
Moderator Action: Ping Content Department for review

GFX / VFX Department

Reviews portfolios and prior work for design/editing roles.

Roles: @VOID GFX, @VOID VFX
Required Proof: Portfolio + examples of previous work
Moderator Action: Ping GFX/VFX Lead for review

Coaching

Applicants must show proof of coaching experience and player results.

Role: @Coach
Required Proof: How many coached + player tracker links
Moderator Action: Escalate to staff for verification

Grinder+ Alternative

If a member does not qualify for other roles, moderators may offer Grinder+. This is optional and still must be documented.
